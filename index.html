    How the data was prepared

    • The master list is the "Preliminary Research of Boards" sheet. Entities that also appear in the "Compilation of Mandals and Mahamandals" sheet carry an M marker and can be isolated with the toggle above.
    • Two Marathi Language Department entries have a blank status in the first sheet; their status (Undetermined) is taken from the second sheet.
    • "Department of Co-Operation, Marketing and Textiles" was merged into "Co-operation, Marketing and Textiles Department", as both spellings refer to the same department.
    • S. No. 39 does not appear in the source sheet.

    Analysis: derived columns

    Legal form and Year est. are not in the source. They were derived from the Statute/Act/Administrative Order text: legal form by keyword (Companies Act, Societies Registration Act, co-operative societies law, a named Act, or a Government Resolution), with four entries corrected by hand after reading the text. The year is the first establishment or incorporation date in that text, ignoring the year of the enabling Act; it is left blank where the text gives only the Act. Check the full statute text before citing either.
